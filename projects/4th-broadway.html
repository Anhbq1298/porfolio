Project

Offices on levels 3–7, a 304-room hotel on levels 9–30, an urban park at ground level, rooftop patios on levels 3 and 9, a pool and bar on level 31 and banquet space on level 32. Two basement levels for parking extend 24 ft below grade; the tower rises 368 ft plus a 32 ft mechanical penthouse.

Column design

  • Gravity. Estimated column forces by tributary area and validated them against ETABS. Used an in-house spreadsheet to find good starting sizes under axial load and to avoid slender columns.
  • Seismic. Designed tower and podium columns for the moments and shears induced by design earthquake displacements (ACI 318-14 §18.4.3.2 and §18.14.3.3), with transverse reinforcement detailed for ductility and confinement.
  • Slab–column connections. Provided the minimum requirements of ACI 318-14 §18.14.5.1 so connections stay effective under large drifts.
  • Verification. Built hand-calculation spreadsheets to check the software results.

Mat foundation

  • Designed the mat in CSI SAFE.
  • Checked soil bearing pressure under the service combinations of CBC alternate equations 16-21 and 16-22.
  • Designed flexural and shear reinforcement for the ultimate combinations (ASCE 7 equations 6 and 7).

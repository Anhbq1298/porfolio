Project

Low-rise concrete office buildings with 50 ft post-tensioned girders for gravity framing and moment frames as the lateral force-resisting system.

Construction administration

  • Reviewed shop drawings and answered RFIs, making sure structural details were clear to the builders.
  • Led a team of 5 — distributing work fairly and checking the quality of each review.
  • Kept close contact with the on-site engineer so construction deadlines were met.

Process improvement

  • Submittal dashboard. Built a Power BI dashboard to track submittal status and team workload.
  • Workbook automation. Refactored hand-calculation workbooks in VBA to automate repetitive tasks such as printing and data handling.

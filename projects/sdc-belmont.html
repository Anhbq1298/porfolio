Structural system

The administration platform uses composite steel framing with a column-free data hall: roughly 54 ft spans on 30 ft bays, with a single central column line in the aisle. The equipment platform has 36 × 34 ft bays; other areas span up to 40 ft.

  • Gravity: composite slab and metal grating on orthogonal steel and composite beams.
  • Lateral: concrete-on-metal-deck diaphragms delivering wind and seismic loads to HSS braced frames.

Approach

  • Gravity framing. Hand-calculated one representative bay, accounting for the architecture and mechanical equipment weights, to set initial sizes; then sized all gravity beams and columns in RAM Structural System.
  • Lateral system. Sized every braced frame in ETABS, designed the slab-on-deck diaphragms, and designed in-plane trusses that act as the equipment-platform diaphragm between brace lines.
  • Piles. Sized all piles for the bidding package and produced a visual pile map — location, diameter and estimated capacity — to coordinate design with drawing production.
  • Verification. Hand-calculation spreadsheets to validate software results.

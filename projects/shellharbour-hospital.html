Scope

Responsible for the floor diaphragm design across the entire project during the construction-documentation phase, from analysis through to reinforcement on the drawings.

Approach

  • Analysis. Extracted diaphragm forces from the ETABS model to identify load paths, transfer zones and areas needing additional reinforcement.
  • Documentation. Prepared Bluebeam mark-ups for the drafting team and carried out QA/QC on the final drawings.
  • Cost control. Monitored reinforcement quantities so the site team had reliable figures for pricing.
  • Change tracking. Worked with the BIM team in Revit to set up a workflow that continuously tracks design changes between model and drawings.

Figures

Outcome

A diaphragm design documented consistently across the project, with checked drawings, tracked reinforcement quantities and a change-tracking workflow shared with the BIM team.

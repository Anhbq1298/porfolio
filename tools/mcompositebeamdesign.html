mCompositeBeamDesign

A composite-beam design environment for conventional and non-typical arrangements: tapered members, FEM-based LTB, variable shear-stud layouts and transverse shear checks.

Problem

Generic composite beam tools often assume prismatic steel members and uniform connectors. Tapered beams, irregular stud arrangements, construction stages and slab shear-transfer checks require more adaptable numerical workflows.

Solution

Combine EC4 design checks with FEM-based elastic critical buckling analysis for non-prismatic beams, configurable shear-connector positions and explicit checks for slab shear transfer and transverse reinforcement.

Engineering methodology

  • Geometry and staging. Define uniform or tapered steel members, slab properties, construction stage and loading.
  • LTB analysis. Use an FEM-based route to obtain elastic critical buckling behaviour for beam geometries beyond standard formula assumptions.
  • Shear connection. Model non-uniform shear-stud arrangements and evaluate connection capacity and the degree of connection.
  • Shear and serviceability. Check vertical beam shear, longitudinal shear transfer in the slab, associated transverse reinforcement and deflection.
  • Traceability. Show the governing checks and export an editable technical calculation into MCalcCanvas.

Engineering outcome

A transparent workflow for composite beam scenarios that do not fit routine uniform-beam and regular-stud assumptions. The detailed solver scope and verification status should remain visible alongside each design check.

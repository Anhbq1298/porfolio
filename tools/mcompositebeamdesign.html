Problem

Composite beam design spans two stages (construction and composite), many clauses and several failure modes. Spreadsheet tools give a pass/fail number but rarely show where it comes from, which makes checking slow.

Methodology

  • Inputs. Steel section from a library (uniform or tapered), slab type (solid or profiled deck), materials, partial factors and loads.
  • Checks. Bending, shear, lateral-torsional buckling, shear connection, web under point loads, transverse slab reinforcement and deflection — each with its clause, location and utilisation.
  • Visuals. Longitudinal section, load diagrams and the plastic stress block at the critical section.
  • Report. One click opens the full calculation in MCalcCanvas for editing and PDF export.

Result

Engineers see the governing check, its clause and its location at a glance, and can hand reviewers a full, editable calculation instead of a spreadsheet.

Technical implementation

  • TypeScript front end running on the MCalcCanvas engine; every result is a worksheet equation.
  • Independent reference calculations used to pin numerical results in unit tests.

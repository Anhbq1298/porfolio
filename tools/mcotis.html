Problem

Choosing the depth of a transfer truss and the pretension of its cables is a coupled problem: each choice changes member forces, deflection and column demand. Doing this by hand in SAP2000 means building, running and post-processing one model per option, so only a handful of options are ever compared.

How it works

Methodology

  • Candidate generation. The engineer defines ranges for loading, truss height and cable pretension; Mcotis builds every combination as a candidate.
  • Analysis. Each candidate is analysed by an in-house FEM solver with tension-only cables, benchmarked against SAP2000, so no external solver licence is needed.
  • Design. Steel, CFT, encased and mega composite columns are checked and optimised over section ranges.
  • Comparison. Material quantities and estimated cost are reported per candidate.

Result

Design teams can compare a full grid of truss depths and cable pretensions on deflection, member utilisation, tonnage and cost in one run, instead of a few hand-built models.

Technical implementation

  • WPF / .NET 8 desktop application with a single-window document workflow.
  • In-house frame and cable FEM solver; SAP2000 and ETABS kept only as import/export utilities.
  • Verification suite comparing solver results against SAP2000 benchmarks.

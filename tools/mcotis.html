Problem

Iterating a cable-supported truss by hand requires repeated analysis models, manual post-processing and disconnected decisions about depth, pretension, serviceability and cost.

Proposed solution

Generate a structured matrix of loading, truss geometry and cable pretension, solve candidate structures with a dedicated FEM engine, and rank design alternatives using comparable engineering outputs.

Engineering methodology

  • Define design space. Engineers select load ranges, truss depth and pretension values.
  • Structural analysis. An in-house FEM solver evaluates each configuration, including tension-only cable behaviour.
  • Design and comparison. Evaluate member responses, column options, material quantities and estimated costs.
  • Verification. Compare representative numerical responses with SAP2000 benchmarks.

Engineering outcome

A repeatable engineering study rather than isolated trial models: alternative configurations can be assessed against deflection, internal forces, material quantity and cost.

Technical implementation

  • WPF / .NET 8 desktop application with a single-window document workflow.
  • In-house frame and cable FEM solver; SAP2000 and ETABS kept only as import/export utilities.
  • Verification suite comparing solver results against SAP2000 benchmarks.

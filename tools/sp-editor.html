Problem

Shear-wall design in spColumn needs geometry and design forces for every pier, transcribed by hand from ETABS. On large buildings this is hours of manual work in the middle of the design process.

Solution

Automate the extraction of wall pier geometry and forces through the CSI API, then generate consistent spColumn inputs and design checks in batches.

Methodology

  • Extract wall-pier geometry and retrieve and filter pier design forces through the ETABS API.
  • Take the engineer's reinforcement pattern as input.
  • Run batch processing and export demand/capacity results.
  • Generate spColumn files for detailed design in later phases (100% DD or CD).

Result

Removed the manual transcription step between ETABS and spColumn. The project received an award from CORE studio in Thornton Tomasetti's R&D 2024 Cycle 1.
